# Open questions

These questions are intentionally unresolved. They form the initial research queue.

## Foundations

1. What is the minimum set of primitives needed to define entity, distinction, relation, and transformation?
2. Are the candidate primitives genuinely independent, or are some definable from others?
3. What counts as an entity in physical, abstract, experiential, and fictional discourse?

## Relations and measurement

4. In what precise sense are connection, relation, and measurement the same or different?
5. Is measurement a special kind of relation, a mapping from cases to values, a procedure, or a family of related concepts?
6. Can every relation be characterized or measured in a meaningful sense? What would falsify or constrain this claim?
7. What is required to measure something when there is no external reference object? Can self-relations or internal distinctions suffice?

## Perspective and meaning

8. What makes two perspectives comparable?
9. Which properties remain invariant when observer, context, language, or available information changes?
10. How can the framework distinguish a represented entity from its representation?

## Truth and stability

11. What exactly would it mean to measure truth rather than evidence, confidence, or robustness?
12. Which transformations should a claim survive for stability to matter?
13. How should the system handle ambiguous claims, context-sensitive claims, and genuine contradictions?
14. Under what conditions does a claim being preserved as an expression differ from the truth of its content?

## Cross-domain unification

15. Which structures recur across physics, psychology, logic, imagination, and ethics?
16. What evidence would show that a shared structure is more than a metaphor?
17. Which domain mappings produce new predictions or explanations?
18. What would count as a counterexample to the overall unification hypothesis?

## Initial milestone

Resolve a precise candidate definition of relation, then test whether measurement can be constructed from relation plus a property, criterion, and result mapping. Preserve competing definitions until the comparison is complete.
