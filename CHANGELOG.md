# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Truth-first research direction

- Made truth and the assessment of truth the first foundational research target.
- Added an investigation protocol separating truth from evidence, confidence, consistency, stability, usefulness, and consensus.
- Added a provisional framework filter for truth status, time, meaning, value, mattering, evidence, provenance, relevance, and revision.
- Specified that filtering is multidimensional and task-dependent, not a universal truth score.
- Clarified that r1's cognitive framework is independent of LLMs; no LLM is assumed to define or supply its core cognition.
- Kept existing primitives, schemas, and architectures provisional.

## 0.1.0 — Initial research structure

- Created repository map and research commitments.
- Recorded initial hypotheses about relational measurement, cross-domain structures, perspective, and truth/stability.
- Added provisional concept notes and a protocol for formalization, testing, provenance, and revision.
- Added a narrow representation prototype and an initial experiment plan.
- No universal theory or completeness claim is asserted.
