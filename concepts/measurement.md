# Measurement

**ID:** C-MEA-001  
**Status:** Working concept; broad thesis under investigation  
**Origin:** Founder hypothesis: “measuring is relating”; measurement need not be limited to length, mass, or other physical quantities.

## Intended meaning

Measurement, in the broad proposed sense, characterizes a property or relation by applying defined criteria. The project investigates whether conventional quantitative measurement is one special case of a more general relational operation.

## Distinctions to preserve

- **Relation:** an association or structure among specified relata.
- **Comparison:** evaluating relata under a criterion.
- **Measurement procedure:** a defined method for producing an observation or result.
- **Measure / result:** the output, which may be numerical, ordinal, categorical, or structured.
- **Interpretation:** what the result means in a domain.

These are related but not automatically identical. The research question is whether they can be derived from a common formal account without erasing their differences.

## Candidate schema

A measurement specification might be represented as:

`M = (X, P, R, T, O, C)`

where, provisionally:

- `X` is the target domain or set of cases;
- `P` is the property being investigated;
- `R` is the relation or comparison criterion;
- `T` is the procedure or transformation applied;
- `O` is the output space (numbers, ordering, categories, or structures);
- `C` is the context and validity conditions.

This is a bookkeeping schema, not a validated universal law. It may need to be replaced or split into several schemas.

## Test for a useful measurement

Specify what is being distinguished, which rule maps cases to results, how results can be interpreted, and what would count as error or disagreement. If no outcome can differ under any conceivable observation or criterion, the proposal may not yet specify an operational measurement.

## Open questions

- Can all meaningful relations be measured, or only characterized?
- Does “measurable in any way” include logical, ordinal, structural, and qualitative characterization?
- How do we prevent “measurement” from becoming synonymous with any description whatsoever?
- Can self-relation support measurement when no external comparator exists?
- Is measurement always relational, while not every relation is a measurement?

## Counterexample discipline

For each candidate definition, test vague, circular, non-discriminating, observer-dependent, and unfalsifiable cases. Lack of a known method is not proof of impossibility, but conceivability alone is not proof of measurability.
